What this does — and doesn't

Complete, not curated

Every ticketed performance is in the statutory record, so nothing is filtered out for being small or regional.

Venue detail that travels

Capacity, halls, wheelchair seating and map links that work in Korea — where Google Maps can't give driving directions.

Links, not transactions

We point at the seller the organiser named. No ticket sales, no payments, no accounts.

No on-sale alerts

KOPIS registers a show only after sales begin, so this site can't tell you when tickets drop. We'd rather say so than pretend.

Performance data provided by KOPIS (Korea Performing Arts Box Office Information System), operated by the Korea Arts Management Service.
